4) Derived databases actually shipped in the installer
-------------------------------------------------------

The files under databases/converted/ are derived databases in the sense of
ODbL-1.0: the thermodynamic TDB was converted to pycalphad syntax and the
compatible mobility parameters of the matching DDB were merged into it. They
are distributed under ODbL-1.0 / DbCL-1.0, like their sources. Every upstream
header, including the copyright and licence notices of both the TDB and the
DDB, is reproduced verbatim inside each derived file.

databases/converted/mc_ni_v2036_with_mobility.garcalc.tdb
    from mc_ni_v2036.tdb + mc_ni_v2012.ddb
    SHA-256: 1882D841A337063E0585D261C690AE7E565838234E231E21B8541A5CB0DBA391

databases/converted/al/mc_al_v2037_with_mobility.thermogar.tdb
    from mc_al_v2037.tdb + mc_al_v2008.ddb
    SHA-256: F9BDF21D434FBE78B5EF3F7F2DE69763FA40B81335CDC58889907D41C80CD717

databases/converted/fe/mc_fe_v2062_with_mobility.thermogar.tdb
    from mc_fe_v2062.tdb + mc_fe_v2016.ddb, plus compatibility patch
    TG-FE-2062-C15-001, which disables one single G parameter of C15_LAVES and
    keeps the original command verbatim as a comment. No value is substituted.
    SHA-256: 236EC4D9B0540DE04E4E6305FAA208672F31FBDF45B2AE84E92F80BD98053612

A per-file conversion record (what was changed, what was disabled and why) sits
next to each file as a .tdb.json passport. Full description: docs/DATABASES.md.


5) Historical note: the pycalphad reference pair
-------------------------------------------------

The conversion rules were originally derived from a pair of mc_ni v2.034 files,
which are no longer part of this repository and are no longer shipped:

    mc_ni_v2.034 original TDB
    https://github.com/by-student-2017/OpenCALPHAD-v6-examples/blob/main/TDB_files/MatCalc6/Thermodynamic_databases_original/mc_ni_v2.034.tdb
    SHA-256: 6ffaf67146f37f9871281356291b5b4c2174b6677a59630c4a17d59fc0befbfd

    mc_ni_v2.034 pycalphad-adapted TDB
    https://groups.google.com/g/pycalphad/c/_WanDJwZGWA
    Attachment published in the pycalphad discussion; UTF-8 version posted by
    Brandon Bocklund on 2021-04-15.
    SHA-256: 9e1b354efcd1c6f9974a7635c0540e545d6375461aaf08c13d9e5a0615145ab6

That discussion warns explicitly that the adapted database had only spot checks
and was not fully validated against MatCalc energetics or physical accuracy.
The pair was used to derive syntax transformations only; every calculation in
ThermoGar is validated independently.
